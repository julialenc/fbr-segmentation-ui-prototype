# Accepted research presentation boundary

The approved screen-and-data specification replaces the prototype's model-first assumptions. Four navigation areas and three detailed family pages are implemented; the wider register is contextual.

- Schema 1.0-presentation enters through SegmentationReport.research and parseReport. Legacy synthetic views remain supported independently.
- Research views need not have segment columns or a fitted model. Each question has its own measurement requirements.
- Claim-known means positive plus documented non-detection; human negative, uncertainty, other unknown, unassessed and market-limited stay separate.
- Counts represent assignments or explicitly labelled pairs. Nested or overlapping populations cannot be summed as unique totals.
- US/Canada remains exploratory. Regional profile percentages are not market prevalence.
- Biscuit metrics are separate median absolute differences across sugar/construction-matched pairs; no new segment is defined.
- Pudding medians are shown for FR/UK only; the single US/Canada qualifier is not interpreted. Sparse communication is insufficient, not 0% or 100%.
- Soda uses sugar-related communication, the saved UK/Irish comparison and its wording exception; not literal sugar-free prevalence.
- Human observations stay qualitative and separate from locked denominators.
- No provider calls, new research, legal qualification or public product examples are introduced.
- Narrative and populations remain in normal mobile page flow. Legacy comparison layouts retain shared formatting.
- No deployment, commit or push is part of this implementation.
